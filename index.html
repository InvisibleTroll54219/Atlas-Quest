How to play

Your expedition guide

Drag the globe to rotate it, then click an unlocked continent pin or region button to begin that quiz. Each quiz has five questions; get four right to clear a continent and unlock the next one.

Clear Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania, and Antarctica to open the World Challenge. Correct answers earn 100 points and build your streak.
