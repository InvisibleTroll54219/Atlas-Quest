How to play

Your expedition guide

Each destination is a five-question round. Get four or more right to clear the level and unlock the next one. Correct answers earn 100 points; build a streak by answering correctly in a row.

Tap Use a hint once per round to remove two wrong choices. It won't affect your score. If you miss the goal, you can try the same level again with a fresh set of questions.

Your cleared levels, best streak, and total score are saved in this browser's server session and a local SQLite database. Reset erases that saved progress.
